9. OpenWings Daily — Offline Android App

This section applies to OpenWings Daily, provided by OpenWings Wellness Pvt. Ltd., with Android package name com.openwingswellness.daily. The app offers guided breathing sessions, daily habits, short wellness activities, saved favourites, and a weekly activity view.

9.1 Information stored and used on your device

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Daily habit selections and their dates, to show which habits you checked off and your weekly activity.
  • Completed breathing-session counts and their dates, to display your completed sessions in the weekly view.
  • Saved activity identifiers, to remember your favourites.
  • Your reduced-motion preference, to remember whether the breathing circle should remain still.

This information is processed on your device for the app's features. The app does not transmit it to OpenWings Wellness or to third parties. We cannot view or retrieve your locally stored activity through the app.

9.2 No account, advertising, tracking, or remote collection

OpenWings Daily works offline and does not require an account or login. It has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, remote-content service, or internet permission. It does not ask for your name, email address, payment details, or health records to use its features. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or Health Connect data.

The app does not include messaging, appointment booking, subscriptions, or payments. References to these features elsewhere in this policy concern our other services, not OpenWings Daily.

9.3 Sharing, sale, and backups

The app does not send or share its locally stored user information with us, advertisers, or other third parties, and we do not sell that local app information. It has no cloud-sync or cloud-recovery feature. Its Android configuration disables app backup and excludes app data from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

9.4 Retention and deletion

Local app information remains on your device until you delete it. To remove your habits, completed-session history, favourites, and preferences, open Preferences & privacy → Clear data in the app and confirm Erase my data. You can also clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Deletion is permanent. We cannot remotely recover or delete the information stored on your device. Updates installed over the same app normally retain local information. Because there is no account, no account-deletion request is required. Clearing local app data does not delete correspondence you separately sent to our support team.

9.5 Voluntary support correspondence

For app support or privacy questions, email info@openwingswellness.com. If you choose to email us, we receive your sender address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to and manage your request. Please do not send sensitive health records or information that is unnecessary for your request.

Email delivery and storage involve the email providers used by you and us. Support correspondence is retained as necessary to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting the same address; any required retention obligations still apply. This correspondence is separate from the local app information, which the app does not transmit.

9.6 Device security

App information is stored in the app's local storage under Android's application protections. Someone with access to your unlocked device may be able to view your activity in the app. Keep your device secure. No storage or security method can guarantee absolute protection.

9.7 Website visits and Google Play

The offline-app statements in this section describe OpenWings Daily itself. They do not mean that visiting this website or using Google Play involves no data processing. When you open this privacy webpage, your browser requests content from our website hosting provider and, where loaded, Google Fonts. These requests can include your IP address, browser information, and the resource requested. Website and other online-service practices are addressed in Sections 1–8. Google Play and your email provider also operate under their own privacy policies.

9.8 General wellness and policy changes

OpenWings Daily provides general wellness activities. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

When the app's privacy practices change, we will update this section and its last-updated date, together with the app's privacy disclosures as appropriate. Contact info@openwingswellness.com if you have questions about OpenWings Daily or this policy.
